What we bake

Traditional Greek patisserie made fresh. Ask in store for today's selection.

  • Baklava

    Almond and cinnamon rolls, Nutella baklava, and pistachio baklava trays.

  • Galaktoboureko

    Filo pastry filled with vanilla custard and soaked in sweet syrup.

  • Spanakopita and savoury pites

    Crisp handmade filo pies: tiropita (cheese), leek and feta, and ham and cheese on weekends.

  • Portokalopita and ravani

    Syrup-soaked orange filo cake and orange semolina cake.

  • Ekmek kataifi and Dubai chocolate cake

    Shredded kataifi layered with custard, pistachio cream and whipped cream.

  • Mini tsourekia

    Sweet braided Greek bread in classic, Dubai pistachio, Oreo, chocolate and Biscoff.

  • Koulourakia and paximathia

    Crunchy Greek biscuits and biscotti in orange, sesame, cinnamon, vanilla and aniseed.

  • Melomakarona and kourabiedes

    Spiced honey-walnut biscuits, and almond shortbread in icing sugar or dark chocolate.

  • Kritsinia

    Fresh Greek breadsticks: cheese, olive, multigrain and mixed sesame.

  • Coffee and celebration cakes

    Espresso drinks, iced coffees, continental cakes, baked ricotta cheesecake, and birthday cakes made to order.

Also in store: pastitsio and moussaka, gourmet sandwiches, gelato, catering, and Greek coffee and pantry items to take home.

About us

Born To Bake is a Greek patisserie and specialty coffee shop in Eastlakes. We make traditional syrup sweets, savoury filo pies, biscuits and small cakes by hand, and we take orders for custom celebration cakes.

Order a cake for a birthday or special occasion by calling or emailing the cafe.
